Stark Eyes — Solar System Atlas

Surface and ring maps (Sun, Mercury–Neptune, Moon and Saturn’s rings):
Solar System Scope, https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/.
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International,

Maps were resized and converted to WebP. The globe previews were rendered
from these maps and use illustrative lighting.

Pluto surface map: PIA19956, NASA / Johns Hopkins University Applied Physics
Laboratory / Southwest Research Institute. This enhanced-color New Horizons
mosaic was resized and encoded as WebP. The globe preview uses illustrative
lighting. The map contains gaps where imagery was unavailable.
https://science.nasa.gov/photojournal/pluto-in-extended-color/

Planet diameters and overview:
